Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

The developer of BoxBell values your privacy and complies with applicable laws.

1. Data We Collect

  • BoxBell works without account signup, and we do not directly collect identifiers such as your name or email address inside the app.
  • Round count, round duration, rest duration, language preference, and other app settings are stored locally on your device to provide app features.
  • Notification permission status may be checked to schedule local notifications.
  • Timer state such as current round, remaining time, and rest status may be provided to the device system for Live Activities and widgets.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Provide boxing round timers, rest timers, and 30-second warnings
  • Play bell sounds and deliver local notifications selected by the user
  • Restore user settings when reopening the app
  • Show timer status on the lock screen, Dynamic Island, and widgets

3. Retention and Deletion

App settings are stored on your device and may be removed when the app is deleted. If you contact us by email, your email address and message may be retained as long as needed to respond and resolve the issue.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not send your timer settings or workout records to a developer-operated server, and we do not sell user data. Information may be provided only when required by law.

5. Permissions and External Services

  • Notifications: Used to deliver round-end, rest-end, and 30-second warning alerts.
  • Live Activities/widgets: Used to show timer status on the lock screen and widgets.
  • Audio playback: Used to play bell sounds bundled with the app.
  • Apple system behavior: Notifications, audio, widgets, and Live Activities follow iOS and Apple system policies.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.
