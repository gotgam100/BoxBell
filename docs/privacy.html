Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 5, 2026

Personal Data We Collect

BoxBell does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, or health data.

On-Device Data

Settings required for app operation, such as rounds, rest duration, language preference, and scheduled alarm identifiers, may be stored on your device. This information is not sent to a developer server.

Alarms and Sounds

BoxBell may use iOS local notifications to notify you of round and rest transitions while the app is in the background. Notification permission and presentation are managed by iOS system settings.
